# 01–08 – Prehistory to 1241

## The landscape: why sites are where they are
Until the Váh was regulated in the mid-19th century, the lowland between the Váh, Dudváh and Šárd flooded regularly. The river left **raised banks (levees) and sand dunes** among side channels, lakes and marshes. These dry islands are where people lived and buried their dead in every period (S031).
The same dunes were dug away for **sand and gravel in the 1950s–70s**. Most sites below were found that way and destroyed: Šaľa-Veča, Galanta, Matúškovo (S032).
