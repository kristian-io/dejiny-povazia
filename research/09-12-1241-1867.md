## Key correction: how the Esterházys got Galanta
The "1421 grant to the Esterházy family" (S002, S008) does not hold up. The family's own history says:
- **Benedek Zerhas de Zerhashaz** (1508–1553), of the Salamon kindred from Csallóköz (Žitný ostrov), married **Ilona Bessenyei de Galántha**. He took over her estates around 1526–1539 (sources differ) and was the first to use the name *Esterházy*.
- Their son **Ferenc Esterházy (1533–1604)** inherited Galanta from his mother and was the first to call himself *de Galántha*. He was vice-ispán of Pozsony County from 1579 and **built the Renaissance manor around 1600**.
- His son **Miklós Esterházy (born in Galanta, 1583; died 1645)** became **Palatine of Hungary** (1625–45), the highest office in the kingdom, and founded the princely line. The Esterházys of Eisenstadt and Fertőd descend from this Galanta family.
- **Update after reading S034 (Galanta's official profile):** in **1421 Mikuláš Estoras, son of Juraj, received the Galanta castle and estates**. He is the first known ancestor of the later Esterházys to hold Galanta. Many families then shared the estate (Péli 1462, Borsi, Bessenyei, Baranyai, Vizkelety, Kajali…). The Esterházys' lasting hold came with the Bessenyei marriage (16th c.) and a **royal grant of 1590 from Rudolf II** to Ferenc Esterházy and others.
- → On the site: "1421 first Esterházy ancestor at Galanta; lasting family seat from the 16th century."
