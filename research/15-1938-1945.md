# 15 – Vienna Award & World War II (1938 – 1945)

## Who ruled here
- State: **Kingdom of Hungary** (regent Horthy), after the **First Vienna Award of 2 Nov 1938**. (S015 says 2 Oct 1938, which is wrong.)
- County: *Nyitra és Pozsony k.e.e. vármegye*, the annexed parts of Nitra and Pozsony counties combined into one
- After the German occupation of Hungary (19 Mar 1944): Hungarian administration under German control
