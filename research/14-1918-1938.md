# 14 – First Czechoslovak Republic (1918 – 1938)

## Who ruled here
- State: **Czechoslovakia**. Czechoslovak troops occupied the area in **January 1919** (Šaľa on 7 Jan 1919); the **Treaty of Trianon (4 Jun 1920)** fixed the border.
- Region: the old counties were kept until 1923, then the Nitra and Bratislava "great counties" (*župy*) until 1928, then the Slovak Land (*Krajina slovenská*)
- District: Galanta district (new, 1923 per S025); Šaľa district
