# 13 – Austria-Hungary (1867 – 1918)

## Who ruled here
- State: **Austria-Hungary**, Kingdom of Hungary half (Franz Joseph I)
- County: **Pozsony County, Galánta district** (Galanta, Matúškovo) / **Nyitra County, Vágsellye district** (Šaľa; district seat in Tornóc = Trnovec nad Váhom)
- Local lords: Esterházy (Galanta), Esterházy & Hennin families (Matúškovo), the University Fund (Šaľa estates)

**Note for the text:** Šaľa's 1880 → 1910 census swing (from 65% Slovak to 92% Hungarian) mostly reflects Magyarisation pressure and how people declared their mother tongue, not migration. The same swing goes the other way after 1919 and again in 1938/1941. It is a strong visual story for the site: a population chart that flips with each change of state.
