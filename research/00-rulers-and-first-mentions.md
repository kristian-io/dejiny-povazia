### A boundary that matters for the map
~~The Váh river roughly separated the two counties.~~ **Corrected (while drawing the map):** the border did **not** follow the Váh. Šaľa, Kráľová nad Váhom, Dlhá nad Váhom, Trnovec and Šoporňa were in **Nitra County**. Diakovce, Tešedíkovo and Žihárec (just south and west of Šaľa), plus Galanta, Matúškovo, Topoľnica and Kajal, were in **Pozsony County**. The line is drawn on the site from municipal boundaries; see [MAP-INVENTORY.md](../maps/MAP-INVENTORY.md).

## C. Conflicts & open questions
1. **Šaľa 1002:** keep "1002" as the traditional date but explain the interpolation; find a scholarly source (e.g. *Pannonhalmi Szent Benedek-rend története*, DHA/Györffy *Diplomata Hungariae Antiquissima*).
2. ~~**Galanta & the Esterházys:** 1421 vs. 1525~~ **Resolved:** the Esterházys got Galanta by marriage (Benedek Zerhas + Ilona Bessenyei, c. 1526–1539). The 1421 date most likely belongs to an earlier owner. See [09-12-1241-1867.md](09-12-1241-1867.md).
3. **Matúškovo 1138 charter:** which document is it, and is it authentic? Check Györffy, *Az Árpád-kori Magyarország történeti földrajza* (Pozsony county volume).
4. **Why "Matúškovo" (1948)?** Ask the municipality, or look in the 1948 renaming decrees and Slovak toponymy literature.
5. **Ottoman control west of the Váh:** was Galanta ever under the Uyvar eyalet, or only raided?
6. **Galanta cemetery: Great Moravian or Hungarian conquest-period?** (Hanuliak & Ižóf 2002 vs. the 10th-c. attribution)
7. **Duslo Šaľa** founding date (commonly given as 1958) needs a source.
8. **Prehistoric cultures and the migration-period sequence** need archaeology sources (Archeologický ústav SAV, Točík 1993, Březinová 1996).
9. **Vágsellye district seat at Tornóc:** confirm, and check whether Šaľa itself was ever the seat before 1918.
