# 16 – Post-war Czechoslovakia & communism (1945 – 1989)

## Who ruled here
- State: Czechoslovakia (restored 1945; Communist Party in power from February 1948)
- Region: districts and regions (*okresy*, *kraje*)
- Local: in Hungarian-majority villages, a commissioner appointed by the Ministry of the Interior at first, not an elected National Committee. Of the Galanta district's 52 communities, only 11 got National Committees at the start.

## Landscape
The Váh is dammed at **Kráľová (1985)**: a 10.9 km² reservoir, a 45 MW hydro plant and a lock. Fields were merged into large cooperative farms.
